## First-release boundary

The core release targets configurable company web/mobile/admin, catalogue and pricing disclosure, favourites/account flows, staff drafts/moderation, enquiry handling, viewing lifecycle and appropriately configured transactional notifications. Each still requires its own delivery evidence and acceptance gates. An item being core scope does not mean it is already implemented or released.

Payments/booking deposits, commission settlement, paid advertising, in-app chat and advanced growth alerts are deferred. This follows the existing business-rules.md pilot exclusion and service-budget.md requirement for separately scoped payment processing, campaigns and paid ads. Standard viewing reminders, configured transactional email, future consented transactional push, direct WhatsApp/call links and staff contact history remain core; they are not advanced marketing/lead-scoring alerts or an in-app chat system.

Displayed refundable deposits and fees remain pricing disclosures, not collection instructions or proof of money received. Staff-reported closed-won outcomes remain staff statements, not payment confirmation. Advertising-authority verification is a private assessment workflow, not a paid advertising product.
