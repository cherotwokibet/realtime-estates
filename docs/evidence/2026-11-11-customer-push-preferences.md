# Customer notification consent and registration ownership, 11 November 2026 EAT

The isolated SQL proposal stores consent for transactional push alerts and synthetic device registration identifiers. Consent defaults off. The database records the latest consent change timestamp and requires the current preference version for updates. Withdrawal deactivates every registration in the same transaction. A later opt-in leaves those registrations inactive until the customer explicitly registers again.

A synthetic registration UUID has one tenant and customer owner across the fixture. Revoking or opting out keeps its owner binding, so another account or company cannot claim it. A customer cannot read another account's preferences or revoke another account's registration. Restricted runtime roles use the scoped SQL functions. The write path locks the current tenant and customer membership in the existing order and checks customer access before changing state. The preview caps each account at ten active and fifty retained registrations. These are local fixture limits pending production retention and device ownership design.
