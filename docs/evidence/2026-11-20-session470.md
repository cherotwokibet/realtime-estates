## Defect and correction

Previously every hosted auth notification meant `auth-changed`, including the notification sent BEFORE a logout POST completed. Other tabs immediately fetched the still-valid cookie session, restoring private data during slow or failed sign-out. Customer, staff and sign-in clients now distinguish signing-out/signed-out invalidation from a successful sign-in notification. Invalidation clears private rows, branch selection/name/reason, receipt/candidate state and sign-in inputs, cancels pending work and invalidates its generation. Focus, foreground and persisted pageshow events cannot automatically reopen the private view after sign-out or expiry. Unknown/legacy notifications fail closed. An explicit recheck or signed-in notification still uses the authoritative session GET; a broadcast never carries authority. Customer pagination state clears too.
