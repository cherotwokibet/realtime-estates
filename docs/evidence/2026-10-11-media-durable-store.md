## Exact limits and next work

Controlled writer exit proves recovery of a committed pair after process exit. It does not prove forced mid-install termination, power loss, directory-entry durability, database crash/restore, decoder restart recovery or production object backup. Partial cases are manually constructed, not observed crash injection. Fresh reopening anchors current file identities; byte-identical replacement beforehand is indistinguishable. Trusted stable local filesystem/same-user access are assumptions. No Windows ACL/network/CPU/native-memory sandbox or hostile same-user race resistance is established.

No deletion, overwrite, repair, lock expiry/stealing, retention or sweeper exists. Incomplete objects expose no bytes and remain reserved; attempt2/3 cannot reclaim a job/kind slot, and a committed output cannot change generation. Concurrent readers may refuse an in-progress receipt; no automatic retry is provided. Admission is per store instance, not a global scheduler. close() drains admitted work without deleting files or cancelling native I/O.

SQL lease expiry never proves native termination. The existing deliberate missing-native-receipt test again retained its unknown attempt and blocked its supervisor. No actual production worker was running. This storage adapter records no worker-attempt journal and authorizes no decoder relaunch. Next design a bounded durable attempt journal, explicit admission/startup reconciliation and narrow private job-context lookup; preserve unknown outcomes across restart. Then exact-version authenticated attach intents/finalize/remove/reorder and verified provenance submission gates.
